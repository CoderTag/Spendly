Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the service, we also store the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your account and expense data to provide the core functionality of Spendly, including displaying your spending history, generating category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party services. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers to host the application and database, but these providers do not have independent access to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided in your account settings or the Spendly support channels.
